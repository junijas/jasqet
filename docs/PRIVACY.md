# Jasqet privacy policy

Jasqet (the app and the "Jasqet Catcher" browser extension) does not collect,
sell or share personal information. It has no ads, analytics or tracking.

**What the extension does with data:** when you download something, the
extension sends the download link, the page address and the cookies for that
site to the Jasqet app running on your own computer (address 127.0.0.1). This
lets downloads that need your login work. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

**What the app sends over the internet:** only the requests needed to download
what you ask for, from the sites you download from. Some features make requests
only once you use them: YouTube support downloads its helper tools from GitHub
the first time you download a video from YouTube or another video site, and
updates them; automatic subtitles download their speech engine from GitHub and its
language model from Hugging Face the first time you make subtitles (the
listening itself happens on your computer: no sound or text is sent
anywhere); VirusTotal look-ups send a file's fingerprint (hash) to
VirusTotal if you add your own key; Telegram alerts go to the Telegram bot you
set up; LAN sharing talks only to other Jasqet computers on your local network.

**Where data is stored:** your download list and settings stay on your computer
(%AppData%\Jasqet). Uninstalling Jasqet leaves your downloaded files and
settings in place; you can delete them yourself.

Questions: contact the person who gave you Jasqet.
